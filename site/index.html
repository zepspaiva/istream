How it works

Choose on the phone. Watch on the TV.

Step 1

Pick on your iPhone

Browse, search and keep your library in a fast, native app. Your list and where you stopped are saved on your phone.

Step 2

Your TV wakes up and plays

Tap Play on TV. The TV turns on, opens Couchwire and starts the video by itself. Subtitles and audio follow your preferences.

Step 3

Your phone is the remote

Pause, skip, change subtitles or the volume from the app, the Lock Screen or the Dynamic Island.

Put the phone down. The TV keeps playing.

The TV plays the video itself, so you can lock your phone, take a call or leave the room. Nothing is streamed from the phone.

Why a plain screen

Your TV already has a great picture. Its software is the problem.

Questions

Before you ask

Does my phone need to stay on while I watch?

No. The TV plays the video itself. The phone only sends the choice and acts as the remote, so you can lock it or walk away.

Do I need an Apple TV, Chromecast or a home server?

No. Couchwire talks to your TV directly over your home Wi-Fi. There is no extra box and nothing to keep running.

Do I need an account?

No. Your library and your progress are stored on your iPhone.

Why does it need LG Developer Mode?

The Couchwire TV app isn't in LG's app store yet, so it's installed directly from your phone. Developer Mode allows that. LG asks you to renew it about every 40 days, and the app will help you do it.

Why isn't it on the App Store?

Couchwire is launching on AltStore PAL, which is available to iPhone users in the EU.

Does it work with my Samsung, Sony or other TV?

Not yet. LG comes first. Pick your TV in the waitlist and we'll tell you when it's supported.
